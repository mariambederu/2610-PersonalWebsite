Portfolio

The goal of this project was assisting Capital One associates in sending messages tailored to customers' level of impact within minutes of learning about a cyber security incident. This is for people who have an account with Capital One and want specific information about their account. We learned to create reusable messages that could be excised without the legal team re-approving them and also looked into expediting the review process. We found that allowing associates to quickly and easily send messages required limiting that ability to certain user roles. We agreed the current User Roles and Permission on the platform needed to change before we went to production with the Emergency Message Feature.
