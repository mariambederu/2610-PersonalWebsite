About me

I'm originally from Addis Abeba, Ethiopia, and currently reside in Washington DC; surrounded by the diverse community, culture and food(!) of the nation's capital. Good design is the result of deep insights into what people really need, feel and desire. Incorporating my broad background in user experience strategy and design; I’m committed to industry best practices, including accessible and inclusive design. In my spare time, I practice other artistic disciplines like painting, jewelry making, and photography.
